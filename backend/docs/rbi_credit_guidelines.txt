RISKLAKE CREDIT RISK GUIDELINES — DOCUMENT 1
RBI Master Circular: Prudential Norms on Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning
Version: 2024-01 | Source: Reserve Bank of India

SECTION 1: DEBT-TO-INCOME RATIO THRESHOLDS

1.1 Standard Norms
The Reserve Bank of India recommends that retail lenders maintain the following Debt-to-Income (DTI) thresholds for new loan originations:

- DTI below 36%: LOW RISK — Loan may be approved under standard terms.
- DTI between 36% and 43%: MODERATE RISK — Loan may be approved with enhanced documentation and income verification.
- DTI between 43% and 50%: ELEVATED RISK — Loan requires senior credit officer approval and additional collateral assessment.
- DTI above 50%: HIGH RISK — Loan should generally be declined unless exceptional mitigating factors exist (e.g., high collateral coverage ratio above 2.0, perfect repayment history exceeding 5 years).

1.2 Employment Type Adjustments
- Salaried employees: Standard DTI thresholds apply as above.
- Self-employed individuals: DTI thresholds are reduced by 5 percentage points due to income volatility.
- Contract workers: DTI thresholds are reduced by 3 percentage points.
- Unemployed applicants: Applications should not be approved unless co-applicant income is sufficient.

1.3 Loan Purpose Adjustments
- Home purchase loans: DTI threshold may be relaxed by up to 5% due to asset-backed nature.
- Business loans: Assessed on business cash flow, not personal DTI alone.
- Personal loans: Strictest DTI thresholds apply — no relaxation permitted.

SECTION 2: CREDIT SCORE REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Minimum Credit Score Thresholds
- Credit score >= 750: Prime borrower. Eligible for all loan products.
- Credit score 700-749: Near-prime. Standard loan products with regular interest rates.
- Credit score 650-699: Sub-prime. Higher interest rates, mandatory credit counselling.
- Credit score 600-649: Deep sub-prime. Loan amount capped at 2x annual income. Requires guarantor.
- Credit score below 600: Generally declined. Refer to secured loan products only.

2.2 Hard Inquiry Policy
- 1-2 hard inquiries in 6 months: Acceptable.
- 3-4 hard inquiries in 6 months: Flag for manual review. May indicate credit shopping or financial distress.
- 5+ hard inquiries in 6 months: Automatic decline recommended unless documented explanation provided.

SECTION 3: COLLATERAL ASSESSMENT

3.1 Collateral Coverage Ratio (CCR)
Collateral Coverage Ratio = Collateral Value / Loan Amount

- CCR >= 2.0: Strong collateral. Can offset elevated DTI or borderline credit score by one tier.
- CCR 1.5 to 1.99: Adequate collateral. Standard risk treatment.
- CCR 1.0 to 1.49: Marginal collateral. No risk offset permitted.
- CCR below 1.0: Undercollateralised. Additional guarantors or co-applicants required.

SECTION 4: DELINQUENCY AND DEFAULT HISTORY

4.1 Delinquency Rate
- Delinquency rate 0%: No adverse history. Full credit eligibility.
- Delinquency rate 1-5%: Minor delinquency. Acceptable with explanation letter.
- Delinquency rate 6-15%: Significant delinquency. Mandatory 12-month clean repayment proof required.
- Delinquency rate above 15%: High delinquency. Loan decline recommended.

4.2 Prior Default
- Single default resolved > 3 years ago: May proceed with elevated interest rate.
- Multiple defaults or default within 3 years: Automatic decline.

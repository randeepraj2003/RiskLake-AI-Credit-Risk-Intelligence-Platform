
RiskLake Credit Risk Guidelines — Internal Reference Document
Version 2.1 | Effective: January 2024

=======================================================================
SECTION 1: LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
=======================================================================

1.1 Minimum Credit Score Requirements
--------------------------------------
- Grade A loans (Low Risk):     Credit score >= 750
- Grade B loans (Medium Risk):  Credit score 650–749
- Grade C loans (Elevated Risk):Credit score 550–649
- Grade D/E loans (High Risk):  Credit score < 550 — requires senior approval

Loans with credit scores below 500 are automatically declined unless
collateral coverage ratio exceeds 2.0.

1.2 Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio Thresholds
-------------------------------------------
The DTI ratio is the primary gating criterion for loan approval:

- DTI < 0.36  : Low risk — standard approval process
- DTI 0.36–0.43: Moderate — requires additional income verification
- DTI 0.43–0.50: Elevated — requires manager sign-off
- DTI > 0.50  : High risk — automatic flag for credit committee review

RBI circular RBI/2023-24/56 mandates that all retail lenders cap
gross DTI at 0.55 for personal and home loans.

1.3 Loan-to-Income (LTI) Ratio
--------------------------------
- LTI <= 3.0  : Standard approval
- LTI 3.0–5.0 : Requires documented income proof
- LTI > 5.0   : Referred to credit committee; rarely approved

1.4 Employment Type Adjustments
---------------------------------
- Salaried (government/PSU): No additional risk premium
- Salaried (private):        +0.5% rate premium if < 2 years at employer
- Self-employed:             Income averaged over 3 ITR filings required
- Contract workers:          LTI capped at 3.0; DTI limit tightened to 0.40
- Unemployed:                Only eligible for secured loans with LTV < 0.5

=======================================================================
SECTION 2: CREDIT BUREAU INTERPRETATION
=======================================================================

2.1 Hard Inquiry Thresholds
-----------------------------
Hard inquiries in the last 6 months signal credit-hungry behaviour:
- 0–2 inquiries: Normal
- 3–5 inquiries: Elevated — add 0.25% to interest rate
- 6+  inquiries: High risk — refer to credit committee

2.2 Credit Utilisation Interpretation
---------------------------------------
- < 30%  : Healthy — borrower manages credit well
- 30–60% : Moderate — monitor for trend
- 60–90% : Stressed — requires income re-verification
- > 90%  : Maxed out — strong default indicator; cap loan at 50% of requested

2.3 Delinquency History
------------------------
- 0 delinquent accounts    : Clean history
- 1–2 delinquent accounts  : Minor concern; require explanation letter
- 3+  delinquent accounts  : Major red flag; automatic escalation to committee
- Any account 90+ DPD (Days Past Due) in last 24 months: Application declined

2.4 Credit Age
---------------
Borrowers with credit history under 12 months are treated as thin-file
customers. Thin-file policy:
- Require 2 additional identity/income documents
- Cap LTI at 2.5
- Apply 10% additional haircut to collateral valuation

=======================================================================
SECTION 3: TRANSACTION BEHAVIOUR SIGNALS
=======================================================================

3.1 EMI Payment Regularity
----------------------------
EMI regularity score (fraction of months with on-time EMI payment):
- Score >= 0.90 : Excellent — positive signal in PD model
- Score 0.70–0.89: Acceptable
- Score 0.50–0.69: Concerning — request 6-month bank statement
- Score < 0.50  : High risk — likely existing debt stress

3.2 Flagged Transaction Rate
-----------------------------
Transactions flagged by the AML/fraud engine:
- Rate < 1%   : Normal
- Rate 1–5%   : Monitor — no action required
- Rate > 5%   : Refer to compliance team before loan approval

3.3 Balance Trend Analysis
---------------------------
A declining average balance over the last 3 months vs prior 9 months
is a negative signal. If balance_trend_flag = 1 (recent balance lower
than historical average), treat as an additional risk indicator and
request salary slip for the last 3 months.

=======================================================================
SECTION 4: RISK GRADE DEFINITIONS (BASEL II ALIGNED)
=======================================================================

Risk Grade | PD Range    | Action
-----------|-------------|------------------------------------------
A          | 0% – 5%     | Approve; standard rate
B          | 5% – 15%    | Approve; +0.25% rate premium
C          | 15% – 30%   | Approve with conditions; +0.75% premium
D          | 30% – 50%   | Senior approval required; +1.5% premium
E          | > 50%       | Credit committee review; collateral required

=======================================================================
SECTION 5: ESCALATION AND OVERRIDE POLICY
=======================================================================

5.1 Automatic Escalations
---------------------------
The following conditions trigger automatic credit committee escalation:
- PD probability > 0.50 (Risk Grade E)
- DTI > 0.55
- 3+ delinquent accounts
- Any 90+ DPD in last 24 months
- Loan amount > INR 50,00,000 (5 crore)
- Combined risk flag = 1 (high DTI + high inquiry simultaneously)

5.2 Manual Override Rules
--------------------------
A credit officer may override a system decline if:
- Collateral coverage ratio > 2.0 AND credit score > 650
- Employer is a PSU/government entity AND DTI < 0.55
- Existing customer with 5+ year relationship AND clean history

All manual overrides must be documented with rationale and countersigned
by a second credit officer.

=======================================================================
SECTION 6: MACRO ECONOMIC ADJUSTMENTS
=======================================================================

6.1 RBI Repo Rate Sensitivity
-------------------------------
When RBI repo rate exceeds 7.0%:
- Tighten DTI threshold by 0.05 (e.g., 0.50 → 0.45 for elevated tier)
- Cap variable rate loans at LTI 4.0

6.2 CPI Inflation Adjustment
------------------------------
When CPI > 6.0%:
- Apply 15% haircut to self-employed income declarations
- Require updated income proof if application is > 60 days old

6.3 GDP and Unemployment Sensitivity
--------------------------------------
- GDP growth < 5%: Increase base PD by 5% across all risk grades
- Unemployment > 8%: Restrict unsecured personal loans to Grade A/B only

BASEL III CREDIT RISK FRAMEWORK

Grade A PD below 5 percent: Minimal risk standard pricing.
Grade B PD 5 to 15 percent: Low moderate risk adjusted pricing.
Grade C PD 15 to 30 percent: Moderate risk enhanced monitoring.
Grade D PD 30 to 50 percent: High risk higher provisions.
Grade E PD above 50 percent: Very high risk full provision.

Early warning: Three hard inquiries in 6 months is high risk.
EMI regularity below 0.5 is stress indicator.
Credit stress score 3 or above means decline recommended.

RBI CREDIT RISK GUIDELINES

DTI below 0.36: Low risk approved.
DTI 0.36 to 0.43: Moderate risk enhanced scrutiny.
DTI 0.43 to 0.50: Elevated risk senior approval needed.
DTI above 0.50: High risk generally declined.
Credit score below 550: High risk additional collateral required.
Home loans minimum score 700. Personal loans minimum 650.
